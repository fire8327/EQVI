About EQVI

EQVI is the Relationship Layer for Banking — an AI-powered lifestyle personalisation platform, embedded directly inside partner banking apps, that turns everyday financial interactions into moments of real, personal relevance.

We believe banking apps shouldn't just show customers what they have. They should understand what customers need — and help meet it, in the moment, without asking them to leave the app they already trust.

EQVI LTD is registered in England and Wales (Companies House number 14060750), with its registered office in London. We work directly with partner banks to design integrations that fit their customers, their compliance requirements, and their existing app experience — nothing about EQVI is one-size-fits-all.
