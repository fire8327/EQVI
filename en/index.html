Testing Methodology

Audience

The testing involved women and men aged 25–55. It included separate audience segments with different interests:

  • cooking and recipes;
  • healthy living and nutrition;
  • clothing and personal style;
  • sports and fitness;
  • personal development;
  • travel;
  • beauty and personal care.

Testing Period and Geography

From August 2022 to July 2026, EQVI was continuously tested and improved through successive Android and iOS releases. The program covered more than 300,000 app installs across Europe, Latin America, the Middle East and CIS markets.

User Acquisition and Analytics

Users were acquired through Meta, Google and TikTok. Aggregated analytics covered the complete journey—from installation and profile completion to recommendation feedback and return visits.

Data Context

The results reflect comparable product cohorts observed through July 2026. Performance may vary by market, audience segment, platform and acquisition channel.

TRUST & COMPLIANCE

Security, Privacy and Responsible AI

Security, privacy and responsible AI controls designed for banking environments.

Data Minimization

Only data necessary for the relevant use case is used, subject to applicable privacy and retention controls.

Pseudonymous Integration

No directly identifying customer data is required for the core integration. Data is exchanged using pseudonymous identifiers and remains subject to applicable privacy controls.

Customer Control

Customers can manage their preferences and profile through controls provided by the bank.

Regional Hosting

Hosting and data residency can be aligned with applicable bank and regional requirements.

Model Governance

AI models, data handling, training use and decision logging are governed according to defined product and privacy controls.

Clear Decision Boundaries

EQVI generates lifestyle recommendations and contextual signals. The bank retains control over customer targeting, product eligibility, pricing and regulated financial decisions.
