1. Introduction

EQVI LTD ("EQVI," "we," "us," or "our") provides an AI-powered lifestyle personalisation layer embedded within partner banking applications. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over it.

We have designed EQVI so that consent, transparency, and control are built directly into the product experience. This policy is the detailed reference that sits behind those in-product controls.

10. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights over your personal data:

• Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

• Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data directly in your settings.

• Erasure: Request full account or specific data category deletion.

• Restrict processing: Turn off a specific data toggle without deleting the account.

• Data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

• Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interest or profiling.
