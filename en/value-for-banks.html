We measure

Customer Engagement Conversion Revenue Retention
Customer Engagement Conversion Revenue Retention

Engagement

Active users, repeat sessions and completed customer journeys.

Conversion

The share of users who complete a defined purchase or banking product action.

Revenue

Attributable merchant commissions and banking product revenue.

Retention

The share of users who return after 7, 30 and 90 days.

Pilot KPIs, measurement periods and attribution rules are agreed with the bank before launch. Results are assessed against an agreed baseline and, where feasible, a comparable control group.

A commercial model tailored to your bank

EQVI’s commercial model combines platform fees with agreed revenue sharing from eligible partner purchases. Pricing, revenue allocation and attribution are defined for each bank and market.

A bank-specific business case is prepared before the pilot, using clearly stated assumptions.

Start with one customer segment and one use case. Together, we define the commercial model, agree measurable KPIs and validate the business case through a controlled pilot.
