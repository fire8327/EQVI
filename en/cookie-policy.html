1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how EQVI LTD uses cookies, software development kits (SDKs), device storage, attribution technologies, push-notification tokens, device identifiers, crash reporting, and similar technologies (together, "tracking technologies") when you visit our website or use the EQVI service.

2. Where This Policy Applies

This policy covers two distinct contexts:

1. Our marketing website (eqvilibria.com), which may use browser cookies and similar web-based technologies.

2. The EQVI service itself, including where accessed through a partner bank's mobile application, which primarily uses mobile SDKs, device identifiers, and similar app-based technologies.

4. Categories of Tracking Technologies We Use

Category Purpose Consent basis / default
Strictly necessary Enable core functionality, security, and authentication Not consent-based; cannot be switched off
Functional Remember your settings and preferences Not consent-based; cannot be switched off
Analytics and performance Understand usage, diagnose errors, ensure reliability Legitimate interest; configurable in settings
Attribution Confirm merchant purchases for attribution/commission Legitimate interest / contract
Push notifications Send relevant service notifications Consent; off until enabled
Marketing Measure effectiveness of marketing content Consent; off until enabled

6. How to Manage Your Preferences

You can manage your preferences for non-essential tracking technologies in the following ways:

1. On our website, using the Cookie Settings control available in the footer.

2. Within the EQVI service, using the toggles available in Data & Privacy settings.

3. For push notifications, using your device's notification settings.

4. For browser-based cookies generally, using your browser's own cookie management settings.
