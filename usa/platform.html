How It Works

Relationship Layer turns everyday banking interactions into moments of genuine relevance.

Embedded directly inside your banking app, the AI assistant listens to what your customers actually need — from what to cook this week to what to pack for a trip — and responds with personalised, AI-generated recommendations across nutrition, fashion, travel, and everyday lifestyle decisions.

There's no separate app to download and no static form to fill in. The AI assistant explains, in plain language, what data it's asking for and why, right at the point it's needed — and your customers stay in control throughout, with the ability to see why a recommendation was made, give feedback, and adjust their preferences at any time.

The result is a banking app your customers open not just to check a balance, but because it understands their life.

How a recommendation happens:

1. A customer shares a preference, goal, or piece of context — directly, or through a connected data source they've authorised.

2. The platform's recommendation engine analyses that input, using only what's relevant to the specific request.

3. A personalised recommendation is delivered inside the banking app, with the reasoning behind it visible on request.

4. The customer can act on it, give feedback, or dismiss it — every interaction improves future relevance.
