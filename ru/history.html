EQVI History

EQVI's story didn't start with EQVI.

For more than twenty years, our team has built loyalty and lifestyle ecosystem products for major financial institutions — long before "embedded finance" became an industry term. That work took different forms and different names along the way: Vantage Club, Eqvilibria Club, EQVILIBRIA.RU, and EQVI Labs each represent a chapter in the same underlying pursuit — using data and personalisation to make financial products feel more human.

EQVI is the current chapter: the same operating experience and lessons, rebuilt as an AI-native platform and purpose-built to embed inside modern banking apps as their relationship layer.

We're not a startup guessing at what banks and their customers want. We're a team that has been building, testing, and refining this model for two decades — now applying it with the AI capability to do it at a level of relevance that wasn't possible before.
