Value for Banks

Banking apps have become utilities — opened to check a balance, then closed. EQVI gives banks a reason for customers to come back, and a new dimension of relationship beyond the transaction.

For your customers:

1. Recommendations that feel personal, not promotional — grounded in context the customer chooses to share.

2. Full transparency and control over what's used and why, with the ability to adjust or withdraw at any time.

For your bank:

1. A differentiated app experience without building AI, recommendation, or lifestyle-content capability in-house.

2. A new lens on customer engagement and retention, built on relevance rather than notifications and offers.

3. New, non-lending revenue paths through merchant and fulfilment partnerships, with attribution and commission terms agreed transparently with your bank.

4. A partner who has spent over two decades building loyalty and lifestyle ecosystems for financial institutions — so the product decisions behind EQVI are informed by what has and hasn't worked in this space before.

Every integration starts with a conversation about what "relevant" means for your specific customer base — not a fixed feature list.
